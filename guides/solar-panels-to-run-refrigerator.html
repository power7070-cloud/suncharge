How many solar panels do I need to run a refrigerator?

About 400 W of panels for a typical fridge, plus a battery of at least 1 kWh to carry it overnight.

A full-size fridge uses ~1.5 kWh/day. With 5 peak sun hours and ~75% real-world efficiency, each 100 W of panel yields ~375 Wh/day, so you need ~400 W (four 100 W or two 200 W panels). In cloudy weather or short winter days, plan on 600 W.

Estimates only; check your unit's manual and nameplate wattage.
