How long to charge an EcoFlow DELTA 2 with a 200W solar panel?

About 7 peak sun hours, or roughly 1.5–2 sunny days. The DELTA 2 holds 1024 Wh. A 200 W panel typically delivers ~150 W in real conditions (≈75% efficiency), so 1024 ÷ 150 ≈ 6.8 hours of full sun.

In Houston (~5 peak sun hours/day) that is about 1.4 days from empty; in cloudy weather it can take several days. Two 200 W panels (400 W, within the DELTA 2's 500 W solar input) cut it to ~3.5 sun hours.

Estimates only; check your unit's manual and nameplate wattage.
