    FAQ

    Why don't I get the full panel wattage?

    Heat, angle, clouds and MPPT losses mean most portable panels deliver 65–80% of rated watts.

    What are peak sun hours?

    The equivalent hours per day of 1000 W/m² sunlight. Houston averages ~4.5 yearly, less in winter.

    Can I add more panels than the input limit?

    Extra watts above the station's max solar input are clipped (wasted), though slight oversizing helps on cloudy days.
